## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏦 [Banking Transaction Analytics](https://github.com/Ghun06/YOUR-BANKING-REPO) · *10/2026 – 11/2026*
End-to-end analysis of **20,000 banking transactions (8,025 customers)** in Python (Pandas, SciPy, mlxtend, Plotly).

* Found **Loan Payments drive ~60% of fee revenue** and low-income customers bear **4.4x** the fee-to-income burden of high-income ones (Pareto, Burden Index).
* Identified **Champions (19.6% of customers) generating 34.8% of value** and an At Risk group (~980 customers, ~18% of value) for win-back (RFM).
* Revealed **91.5% of repeat customers receive 2+ different offers** and one-month retention is only **~7.7%** (Cohort, Market Basket, statistical tests).
* Evaluated 8 cities, 4 channels, and 32 service points with ABC and Pareto: city performance varies by only ~7% and channels show no significant behavioral differences.

`Python` `Pandas` `SciPy` `mlxtend` `Plotly` `Google Colab`
